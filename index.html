Frequently Asked Questions

At what age is a dog considered a Senior?

It depends on size: giant breeds (Great Danes, Mastiffs) reach senior status around age 5 to 6, large breeds around age 7, and small toy breeds around age 10 to 11.

How many times a day should adult dogs eat?

Vets recommend feeding adult dogs twice daily (morning and evening). Feeding once a day can lead to acid reflux and increases the risk of Gastric Dilatation-Volvulus (bloat) in deep-chested dogs.
